# Assets / Rasilimali

`assets/` hapo sasa ina **placeholder** zilizotengenezwa na jenereta (models za masanduku, textures za rangi,
materials, fonts, animations, glTF za watu). Game inaweza kujengwa na kuendeshwa; muonekano ni wa muda.
Ukiwa na assets halisi za Death Race (haki zako), zibadilishe ndani ya `assets/` kwa majina yale yale.
Note: `.shape` -> `.tres` na `.anim` -> `.tres` (marejeo yamerekebishwa kwenye .tscn).
